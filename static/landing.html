RansomTrace
Automated Ransomware Forensic Analysis & Threat Intelligence Correlation
RansomTrace is an Integrated Digital Forensic Response Framework prototype designed to automate the triage, analysis, and correlation of ransomware-related artifacts. It is capable of taking raw memory dumps or network traces, calculating evidence integrity metrics, and querying internal threat intelligence dictionaries to attribute malicious behavior.
